What should I include?

Direct costs associated with producing or delivering what the business sells.

  • Raw materials, components or stock for resale.
  • Packaging and production consumables.
  • Job-specific subcontractors.
  • Direct production or delivery labour where treated as a direct cost.

Simple test: Is this cost directly attributable to producing or delivering what you sell?

Use the same classification as the business's accounts or management information wherever possible. If unsure, check with the accountant.

What should I include?

General costs of running the business that are not directly attributable to an individual product, job or sale.

  • Administration and management salaries.
  • Premises, insurance and professional fees.
  • Software, IT, telephone and general marketing.

Simple test: Would the business still have most of this cost if sales temporarily fell?

Use the same classification as the business's accounts or management information wherever possible. If unsure, check with the accountant.
